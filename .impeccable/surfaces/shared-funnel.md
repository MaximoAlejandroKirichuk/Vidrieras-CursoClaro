# Shared institute sales journey

User-approved replacement of the three distinct storefront compositions. Store and course pages are Persuade; payment and confirmation are Operate. This brief supersedes the old per-route composition contracts for the active funnel.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Four separate pages make the buying sequence legible: store, course, payment, confirmation. Offer and price precede schedule selection.

**OWN-WORLD:** Preserve each institute's current palette and font families, including Alondra's locally edited jade. Shared ruled catalog rows, generous headings and a plainly labeled booking summary replace repeated marketing cards.

**STORY:** Find a course, understand its existing offer, select a commission and payment plan, then see a clear simulated receipt.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Store: identity and compact introduction followed by open course rows. Course: large title and summary beside start date, installment price and enrollment action. Payment: form with adjacent live financial summary. Confirmation: receipt with start and next due date.

**FORM:** User-pinned four-page reusable funnel; code-led implementation. Preserve native course/group filtering, existing content and simulated enrollment. No invented evidence, photos or integrations.

**FINISH:** Build and behavior verification, responsive review where browser tooling is available; user screenshots will drive subsequent visual refinement. No new raster assets.

# Bisagra course and reservation experience

Mode: Persuade. Audience: prospective students comparing practical courses, dates, locations, and payment options inside a clearly disclosed fictional demo.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A practical course sheet that leads to choosing a real scheduled group, replacing the hero–metrics–cards–testimonials landing sequence.

**OWN-WORLD:** Bisagra's Barlow and Barlow Condensed, workshop orange, safety yellow, white and ink. A dominant orange intensive sheet with plain factual columns and a yellow reservation strip; other courses occupy open, expandable rows. No gear illustration, fabricated proof, hard shadows, or black-neon card grid.

**STORY:** Understand the one-day intensive, compare longer courses and included materials, then select a date and location before exploring simulated checkout.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact wordmark/navigation, one short introductory line, then a wide intensive course sheet. Its course name and practical task lead; duration and materials sit underneath. A yellow strip joins the earliest available date, derived seña, and visible “Ver fechas del intensivo” action. The longer-course index starts immediately below, not another hero.

**FORM:** User-pinned, approved practical course-and-reservation direction; code-led, no generated comp. No concept tournament: composition and brand constraints are already approved.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Delivery boundary

Only the route, its stylesheet, and this brief belong to this work unit. Shared picker and checkout remain authoritative for selection, prices, optional kits, and simulated enrollment. Parent owns browser verification, audit, finish review, and broader documentation. No raster assets required. No unresolved design choice.
